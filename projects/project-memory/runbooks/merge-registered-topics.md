# Runbook

## Purpose

Consolidate multiple registered topics into one when separate semantic units or authority boundaries are no longer justified.

## Procedure

- Confirm the candidate topics should no longer remain separate semantic units or authority boundaries and that the operation is not better modeled as a boundary change on only one topic.
- Choose the merge target topic and define the resulting scope and authority boundary explicitly.
- Update the taxonomy registry so predecessor topics are marked with their merge outcome and the surviving topic becomes the single active authority surface for the merged area.
- Review predecessor canonical documents and decide which document remains authoritative, which ones are superseded, and whether any new consolidated canonical document is required.
- Preserve aliases or historical references where necessary so old lookups remain understandable without leaving multiple active authority surfaces.
- Run a taxonomy audit after the merge to confirm that only one active authority surface remains for the merged semantic area.

## Verification

- Confirm the surviving merged topic and authority boundary are explicit.
- Confirm predecessor topics were retired or superseded intentionally rather than left ambiguously active.
- Confirm aliases or historical references preserve understandable lookup continuity where needed.
- Confirm only one active authority surface remains for the merged area.
